Who Certiplot is for

Buyers and investors

Shortlist areas on facts, see what the State says a square metre is worth next to what contracts declared, check flood, fire, seismic and protected-area exposure, and take a list of questions to the viewing, the lawyer and the engineer.

Estate agents

Answer a buyer's questions with sources in the meeting: area reports under your own name, building activity around a listing, and the official price markers for the conversation about price.

Valuers and banks

Zone price, declared contract prices, the official house-price indices and the rules that apply - each with its date and source, ready to cite in a valuation file.

Engineers, architects and surveyors

A site desk: the constraints that touch a site (seismic zone, flood-risk and burnt areas, Natura 2000 and designated areas), recent building permits and planning acts nearby, a WGS84 / EGSA87 / CGRS93 / UTM coordinate converter, and GeoJSON or KML export for CAD and GIS.

Data and research teams

One documented API over the official statistics of Greece and Cyprus, with the licence and the source file of every series, and a tamper-evident ledger of each build.

All prices on this site are official figures published by public bodies - the State's zone prices (objective values), prices declared in purchase contracts and official price indices. They are not current market or asking prices: the price you pay is agreed with the seller.
This page is general information, not legal, tax or engineering advice. Before you sign, have a lawyer check the title and a licensed engineer check the building.
