What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Amaroussio?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Amaroussio ranges from €1,500 to €2,300 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Amaroussio?

Contracts for apartments in Amaroussio declared about €2,167 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Amaroussio in a flood-risk area?

About 29 % of the area of Amaroussio lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Amaroussio?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Amaroussio (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Amaroussio in?

Amaroussio is in seismic hazard zone I of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Amaroussio?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
