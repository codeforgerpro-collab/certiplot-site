What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Lefkada?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Lefkada ranges from €600 to €1,400 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Lefkada?

Contracts for apartments in Lefkada declared about €1,521 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Lefkada in a flood-risk area?

No officially declared area of potential significant flood risk was found in Lefkada (2018).

Has land burnt in wildfires in Lefkada?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Lefkada (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Lefkada in?

Lefkada is in seismic hazard zone III of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Lefkada?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
