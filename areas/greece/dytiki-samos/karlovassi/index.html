What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Karlovassi?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Karlovassi ranges from €450 to €1,050 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Karlovassi?

Contracts for apartments in Karlovassi declared about €696 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Karlovassi in a flood-risk area?

No officially declared area of potential significant flood risk was found in Karlovassi (2018).

Has land burnt in wildfires in Karlovassi?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Karlovassi (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Karlovassi in?

Karlovassi is in seismic hazard zone II of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Karlovassi?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
