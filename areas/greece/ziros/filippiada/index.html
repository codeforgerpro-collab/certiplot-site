What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Land here has burnt in wildfires since 2016: burnt forest land cannot be built on or rezoned - check the plot's status with the forest service and the engineer.
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Filippiada?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Filippiada ranges from €450 to €850 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Filippiada?

Contracts for apartments in Filippiada declared about €1,049 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Filippiada in a flood-risk area?

About 39 % of the area of Filippiada lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Filippiada?

Wildfires since 2016 burnt land equal to about 21 % of the area of Filippiada (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Filippiada in?

Filippiada is in seismic hazard zone II of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Filippiada?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
