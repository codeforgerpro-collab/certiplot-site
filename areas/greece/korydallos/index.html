What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Land here has burnt in wildfires since 2016: burnt forest land cannot be built on or rezoned - check the plot's status with the forest service and the engineer.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Korydallos?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Korydallos ranges from €1,050 to €1,400 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Korydallos?

Contracts for apartments in Korydallos declared about €1,286 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Korydallos in a flood-risk area?

About 35 % of the area of Korydallos lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Korydallos?

Wildfires since 2016 burnt land equal to about 2 % of the area of Korydallos (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Korydallos in?

Korydallos is in seismic hazard zone I of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Korydallos?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
