What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Land here has burnt in wildfires since 2016: burnt forest land cannot be built on or rezoned - check the plot's status with the forest service and the engineer.
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni ranges from €1,350 to €10,800 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni?

Contracts for apartments in Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni declared about €3,984 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni in a flood-risk area?

About 29 % of the area of Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni?

Wildfires since 2016 burnt land equal to about 8 % of the area of Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni in?

Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni is in seismic hazard zone I of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Vari-Voula-Vouliagmeni?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
