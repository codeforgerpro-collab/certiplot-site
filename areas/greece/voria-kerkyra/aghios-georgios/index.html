What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Aghios Georgios?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Aghios Georgios ranges from €650 to €1,150 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Aghios Georgios?

Contracts for apartments in Aghios Georgios declared about €2,049 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Aghios Georgios in a flood-risk area?

No officially declared area of potential significant flood risk was found in Aghios Georgios (2018).

Has land burnt in wildfires in Aghios Georgios?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Aghios Georgios (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Aghios Georgios in?

Aghios Georgios is in seismic hazard zone II of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Aghios Georgios?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
