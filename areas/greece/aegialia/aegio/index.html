What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Aegio?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Aegio ranges from €500 to €1,150 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Aegio?

Contracts for apartments in Aegio declared about €1,096 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Aegio in a flood-risk area?

About 14 % of the area of Aegio lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Aegio?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Aegio (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Aegio in?

Aegio is in seismic hazard zone II of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Aegio?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
