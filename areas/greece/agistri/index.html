Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Agistri?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Agistri ranges from €550 to €600 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Agistri?

Contracts for apartments in Agistri declared about €1,409 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Agistri in a flood-risk area?

No officially declared area of potential significant flood risk was found in Agistri (2018).

Has land burnt in wildfires in Agistri?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Agistri (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Agistri in?

Agistri is in seismic hazard zone II of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Agistri?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
