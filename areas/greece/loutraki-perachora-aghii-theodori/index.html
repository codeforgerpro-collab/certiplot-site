What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Land here has burnt in wildfires since 2016: burnt forest land cannot be built on or rezoned - check the plot's status with the forest service and the engineer.
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori ranges from €800 to €1,850 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori?

Contracts for apartments in Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori declared about €1,499 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori in a flood-risk area?

About 16 % of the area of Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori?

Wildfires since 2016 burnt land equal to about 13 % of the area of Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori in?

Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori is in seismic hazard zone II of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Loutraki - Perachora - Aghii Theodori?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
