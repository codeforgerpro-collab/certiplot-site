What to check before buying here

  • Ask for the KAEK, the title deed, the engineer's certificate and the energy certificate (PEA).
  • Land here has burnt in wildfires since 2016: burnt forest land cannot be built on or rezoned - check the plot's status with the forest service and the engineer.
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

What is the official zone price in Monemvassia?

The State's zone price (objective value) in Monemvassia ranges from €600 to €3,650 per square metre (2022). It is a tax base, not a market price.

What price per square metre do contracts declare in Monemvassia?

Contracts for apartments in Monemvassia declared about €767 per square metre in 2025, according to the official records.

Is Monemvassia in a flood-risk area?

No officially declared area of potential significant flood risk was found in Monemvassia (2018).

Has land burnt in wildfires in Monemvassia?

Wildfires since 2016 burnt land equal to about 0.6 % of the area of Monemvassia (EFFIS, 2026).

What seismic zone is Monemvassia in?

Monemvassia is in seismic hazard zone I of the official zoning.

What should I check before buying a property in Monemvassia?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
