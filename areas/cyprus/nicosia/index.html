What to check before buying here

  • Ask whether a separate title deed exists, and have your lawyer get a Lands Office search certificate.
  • Land here has burnt in wildfires since 2016: burnt forest land cannot be built on or rezoned - check the plot's status with the forest service and the engineer.
  • Part of the area is in Natura 2000: works and new buildings there may need an environmental assessment.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.
  • Part of this unit lies beyond the line of government control or in the buffer zone: confirm the plot's side with the District Lands Office.

Questions buyers ask

Is Nicosia in a flood-risk area?

No officially declared area of potential significant flood risk was found in Nicosia (2018).

Has land burnt in wildfires in Nicosia?

Wildfires since 2016 burnt land equal to about 2 % of the area of Nicosia (EFFIS, 2026).

What should I check before buying a property in Nicosia?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
