What to check before buying here

  • Ask whether a separate title deed exists, and have your lawyer get a Lands Office search certificate.
  • Part of the area lies in a declared flood-risk area: ask the engineer whether the plot does, and check what the insurer will cover.
  • Check that the building's structure was designed for its seismic zone, or strengthened since.

Questions buyers ask

Is Mesogi in a flood-risk area?

About 1 % of the area of Mesogi lies inside officially declared areas of potential significant flood risk (2018). Ask the engineer whether the plot itself does.

Has land burnt in wildfires in Mesogi?

No land burnt by mapped wildfires since 2016 was found in Mesogi (EFFIS, 2026).

What should I check before buying a property in Mesogi?

The title (by a lawyer), the building's legality and energy certificate (by an engineer), and the area's official figures above. Our buyer checklist lists every step.
