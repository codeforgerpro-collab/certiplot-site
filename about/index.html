About Certiplot

Certiplot is made by Tekmerion in Athens. It was built by a real estate adviser who wanted every figure in a buyer's file to come with its source.

It reads only official, public data - statistical offices, central banks, ministries, cadastres and European agencies - and keeps the date, the licence and the source file of each figure. Where no official figure exists, it says so.

Credits: Antonios Vlachos & the CodeForge team.

All prices on this site are official figures published by public bodies - the State's zone prices (objective values), prices declared in purchase contracts and official price indices. They are not current market or asking prices: the price you pay is agreed with the seller.
This page is general information, not legal, tax or engineering advice. Before you sign, have a lawyer check the title and a licensed engineer check the building.
