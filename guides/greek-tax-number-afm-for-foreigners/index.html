Greek tax number (AFM) and bank account for foreign buyers

Why a foreign buyer needs a Greek tax number (AFM) and bank account, how to get them in person or through a lawyer, and what to have ready.

Why you need them

Every party to a Greek property contract needs a Greek tax identification number (AFM). The price is normally paid through a Greek bank, which also documents where the money came from.

How to get the AFM

  • In person at a tax office, with your passport or ID card and proof of address.
  • Or through a lawyer in Greece holding your power of attorney.
  • Depending on where you live, you may need a tax representative in Greece - your lawyer will tell you.

The bank account

Greek banks ask for your passport, the AFM, proof of address and proof of income or of the source of the funds. Opening it can take time - start early.

Questions buyers ask

Can I get a Greek AFM online from abroad?

Online services exist for some cases; most foreign buyers do it through their lawyer with a power of attorney.
