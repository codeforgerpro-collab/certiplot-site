Buying in Cyprus: title deeds and the search certificate

Why separate title deeds matter in Cyprus, what the Lands Office search certificate shows, why the sale contract is deposited, and what to check near the buffer zone.

Title deeds

In Cyprus the Department of Lands and Surveys issues the title deed. Many properties were sold before separate title deeds existed, sometimes with the developer's mortgage on the whole plot. Ask whether a separate title deed exists for the unit you are buying.

The search certificate

A search at the District Lands Office shows the registered owner and any mortgages, memos or prohibitions. Your lawyer obtains it before the contract and again before completion.

Deposit the contract

The sale contract should be deposited at the District Lands Office promptly after signing; it protects your right to the property against later sales or charges by the seller.

Areas near the buffer zone

Some municipalities border the area outside government control. Certiplot shows, from the Department's own lines, how much of each unit lies on each side - but no line places a single plot: ask the Lands Office.

Questions buyers ask

Can I buy property in Cyprus without a title deed?

It happens, but it carries risk: your lawyer must check the developer's mortgage and deposit the contract.
