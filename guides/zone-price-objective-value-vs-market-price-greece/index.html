Zone price (objective value) vs market price in Greece

What the Greek zone price is, why it is not a market price, how it affects the tax you pay, and how to read it next to contract prices and the official index.

What it is

The zone price is the value per square metre the State sets for each zone, used for transfer tax, ENFIA and other taxes. Transfer tax is calculated on the higher of the contract price and the objective value.

Why it differs from the market

Zone prices are revised rarely; the market moves every quarter. A contract price far below the market but at the zone price is a question for your lawyer, not a bargain.

How Certiplot shows it

Questions buyers ask

Is the zone price what I will pay?

No. It is a tax base; the price is agreed with the seller.
