Golden Visa Greece: a property checklist before you invest

What to check on a property bought for the Greek Golden Visa: the threshold for the address, the title, the engineer's certificate, the real value and the risks of the area.

The rules change - check them first

The minimum investment depends on the area and the kind of property, and it was raised in 2023 and 2024. Ask your lawyer for the threshold that applies to the exact address on the day you sign.

The property

  • A clean title and an engineer's certificate, as for any purchase.
  • A value you can defend: compare the zone price, contract prices and the official index.
  • The area's exposure to flood, wildfire and protected-area rules.

Questions buyers ask

What is the minimum property investment for the Greek Golden Visa?

It depends on the area and the type of property and has changed several times; your lawyer should confirm the figure for the exact address.
