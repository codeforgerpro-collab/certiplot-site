Building permits near a property: what is being built next door

How to see the building permits issued around a property in Greece, and why new construction next door matters for views, light, noise and value.

Why it matters

The plot opposite may be empty today and a five-storey block next year. Building permits are public: they are issued through the Technical Chamber's e-Adeies system, and related acts are posted on Diavgeia.

What to look at

  • New building permits in the last year near the property.
  • Demolition permits: an old house today, a new building tomorrow.
  • The use: homes, shops, a hotel - each brings different traffic and noise.

Questions buyers ask

Are Greek building permits public?

Yes, their basic data are public.
