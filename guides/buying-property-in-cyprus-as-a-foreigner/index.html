Buying property in Cyprus as a foreigner: permission, steps and checks

How EU and non-EU buyers acquire property in Cyprus: the permission non-EU buyers need, the steps from reservation to transfer, and what to verify first.

EU and non-EU buyers

Citizens of the EU buy on the same terms as Cypriots. Buyers from outside the EU need permission to acquire immovable property, applied for through the District Office; it is usually granted for a home, and your lawyer files it.

The steps

  • A reservation, then the search certificate and checks by your lawyer.
  • The sale contract, stamped and deposited at the District Lands Office.
  • Payment and, when the title deed exists, the transfer at the Lands Office.

What to verify first

  • Whether a separate title deed exists, and any developer's mortgage.
  • The planning zone and building coefficients of the plot.
  • Flood, wildfire and protected-area exposure of the area.

Questions buyers ask

Do non-EU citizens need permission to buy property in Cyprus?

Yes, permission is needed for non-EU buyers; it is applied for through the District Office and is usually granted for a home.
