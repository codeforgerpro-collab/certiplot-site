Where the figures come from

Certiplot reads public data only. The app lists every file with its licence; these are the bodies.

  • Hellenic Statistical Authority (ELSTAT)
  • Bank of Greece
  • Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE)
  • Hellenic Cadastre
  • Technical Chamber of Greece (e-Adeies)
  • Diavgeia (public acts)
  • Earthquake Planning and Protection Organisation (OASP)
  • European Forest Fire Information System (EFFIS)
  • European Environment Agency (Natura 2000, protected areas)
  • Eurostat
  • Statistical Service of Cyprus (CYSTAT)
  • Department of Lands and Surveys of Cyprus
  • Central Bank of Cyprus

All prices on this site are official figures published by public bodies - the State's zone prices (objective values), prices declared in purchase contracts and official price indices. They are not current market or asking prices: the price you pay is agreed with the seller.
